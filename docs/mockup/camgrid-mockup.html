CamGrid Preview

A clickable mock of the app's screens, built from its Compose code: same layout, colors and texts. Video is simulated and the cameras are examples. Click or tap like on a phone, or use the remote (or your arrow keys, Enter, Esc and M) like on the Fire TV.

Keyboard: ←↑→↓ Enter Esc M
Grid plays each camera's grid URL, muted. Moving past the right or left edge flips the page.
Fullscreen stops the grid streams first, then plays the detail URL with sound.
Tile states come from the player: connecting, playing, or offline with a retry countdown.
